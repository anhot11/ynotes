yNotes is a privacy-first, ultra-secure note taking and vault application designed for complete data privacy.

Features:
- Military-grade encryption (AES-256-GCM and LINX CRYPT)
- Image Steganography (hide secret encrypted messages inside normal images using DCT)
- Biometric Home Screen Widgets with 10-second auto-lock and screen-off revocation
- Safe Zone App Launcher (Reverse mode & shortcut manager)
- Self-destructing notes with expiration timers
- 100% Free & Open Source, zero tracking, zero ads, zero internet dependencies
